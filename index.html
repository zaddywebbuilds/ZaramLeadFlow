The standard cold DM asks for a call before it has earned a reply. The business never asked for the pitch, does not know you, and has no reason to spend attention on it. The message is not badly written. It is badly timed.

The method

Do not pitch first.
Ask first. Sell second.

Businesses that answer cold DMs answer because the message cost them nothing to reply to. A real question about their own work takes ten seconds to answer and gives away nothing. That answer is the door.

01 · Open

Ask something
only they can answer

A specific question about their services, their busiest season, what they get asked for most. Never an offer, never a rate, never a calendar link.

02 · Reply

They answer
because it is easy

People talk about their own business without effort. One sentence back and the thread is open, which means you are now a conversation and not a notification.

03 · Pitch

Now the offer
has somewhere to land

You showed interest before you asked for anything. The pitch arrives inside a conversation they started, which is the only place a pitch has ever worked.

Why a human presses Send

The keypress is not a limitation. It is the whole reason this lasts.

Instagram is very good at spotting software that sends on its own. Fully automated DM tools get accounts restricted or disabled within days, and the list dies with the account. LeadFlow is built the other way round on purpose: the AI does the work that does not touch Instagram — reading the sheet, studying the profile, writing the opener, placing it in the box — and a person does the part that does.
