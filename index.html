The standard freelancer cold DM ("Hi, I noticed you could use help with X, I offer Y, want to hop on a call?") gets ignored because it asks for commitment before it earns any trust. The business didn't ask for your pitch. They don't know you. Why would they reply?

The Strategy

Don't pitch first.
Ask first. Win later.

The businesses that reply to cold DMs have one thing in common: the message made them feel like a human was genuinely curious — not hunting for a sale. A well-crafted question about their services, their business, or their customers gets a reply because it takes 10 seconds to answer and costs them nothing. That reply opens the door. Then you walk through it.

1
Send a genuine inquiry LeadFlow writes a unique question about their business — what they offer, what makes them different. Not a pitch.
2
They reply because it's easy Businesses love talking about themselves. A simple question gets answered. Now you're in a conversation.
3
Now you pitch — and it lands You're no longer a stranger. You showed curiosity. They're warm. That's when you introduce what you do. And it works.
